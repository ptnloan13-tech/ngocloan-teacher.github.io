Teaching approach

How I teach

Four ideas guide every lesson I plan.

Affective filter

Low stress, real rapport

Learners take risks when the room feels safe. I build a relaxed, supportive and fun classroom first.

CLT · TBLT

Language for doing things

Students use English to complete real tasks, not just to fill in blanks.

Scaffolding

Peers and autonomy

Well-scaffolded pair and group work builds skills and confidence step by step.

EdTech

Technology with a purpose

Digital tools make every voice visible in a big class and keep the energy up.

Every lesson should help learners enjoy and succeed in their language journey.
